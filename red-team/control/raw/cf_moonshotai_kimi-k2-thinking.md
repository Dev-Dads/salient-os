**Steel Man (what is genuinely right)**

The escalation logic is robustly upward-only: `max_stakes` uses an explicit total order and treats any malformed input as “absent” (rank ‑1), ensuring `effective_stakes` can never be lower than the policy-signed `envelope.stakes`. The `decide` gate correctly composes the two policy floors (directive depth and stakes floor) with `max()`, guaranteeing salience can only raise the bar. The adaptation sealed gate strictly requires `verdict.status is Status.VERIFIED`, preventing any laundering of `INTEGRITY_ATTESTED` or merely-cleared actions into learning. The verdict’s self-describing design (`envelope_id` + `effective_stakes` stamped by `verify`) eliminates free-parameter desynchronization attacks that plagued earlier drafts.

**Verdict**

**SERIOUS_FLAWS** – The `decide()` gate, while logically correct on well-formed inputs, lacks defensive validation against malformed `Directive` or `Verdict` objects (non-integer depths, unhashable stakes). Under the in-scope adversary (buggy caller, non-malicious corruption), these inputs cause unhandled `TypeError` exceptions, violating the fail-closed requirement (Invariant 2) by crashing rather than denying clearance. The fixes above (minimal type guards) are sufficient to restore the invariant.
